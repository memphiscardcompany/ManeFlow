# ManeFlow — Microsoft Store Listing Draft

**Product name:** ManeFlow

**Publisher:** Memphis Card Company LLC

**Category:** Business / Productivity

## Short description

Identify cards and mixed lots, evaluate buying opportunities, manage inventory, process Ricoh duplex scans, track grading, and make evidence-based pricing decisions.

## Full description

ManeFlow is a card intelligence and operations platform built for collectors, dealers, and high-volume card businesses. It combines single-card scanning, mixed-lot analysis, bulk scanner intake, collection management, grading workflows, pricing confidence, and sales preparation in one application.

ManeFlow does not invent market values. Pricing is shown only when sufficient authorized evidence exists, with comp counts, confidence, and data freshness displayed alongside recommendations.

## Core features

- Single-card and slab recognition
- Multi-card and eBay lot opportunity analysis
- Ricoh duplex bulk intake and front/back pairing
- Vault, grading queue, corrections, and inventory controls
- Conservative sold-comp valuation and dealer decision tools
- Offline-friendly desktop operations
- Opt-in recognition learning with private business data excluded
