## Description

ManeFlow helps collectors and shops identify trading cards, review source-labeled completed-sale comps, track collection and inventory performance, watch price targets, and prepare pricing or consignment decisions.

ManeFlow is built around transparency. It shows value confidence, comp quality, image source status, scan confidence, cert status, and why a card may need manual confirmation before action.

Values are estimates based on included completed-sale comps. Active asking prices are context only. ManeFlow is not an appraisal, authentication service, grading opinion, guarantee, tax advisor, legal advisor, or investment advisor. Public live-market claims require authorized completed-sale data feeds to be connected and healthy.
