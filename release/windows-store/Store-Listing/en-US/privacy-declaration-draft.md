# ManeFlow privacy declaration — operational draft

ManeFlow may process account identity, uploaded trading-card images, card metadata, scan results, correction records, collection records, grading/service records, and technical diagnostics needed to operate the service.

Card images and metadata may be transmitted to hosted recognition services to identify cards and extract visible information. Official data providers such as PSA or eBay may receive the minimum query information needed for a requested lookup. Provider credentials are stored server-side and are not included in the Windows package.

Corrections are private by default. A card crop and sanitized identity label are added to ManeFlow's recognition-learning data only when the user explicitly opts in. Private inventory values, purchase cost, customer information, and pricing records are excluded from that learning record.

Users should be given controls to export and delete their account data. The production privacy policy must list current subprocessors, retention periods, contact information, applicable state/country rights, and the exact account-deletion procedure.
