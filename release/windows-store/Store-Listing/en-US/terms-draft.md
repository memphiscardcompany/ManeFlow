# ManeFlow terms — operational draft

ManeFlow provides card identification, collection-management, workflow, and market-research tools. Recognition results and prices are informational estimates and may be incomplete or incorrect. Users must review the physical card, certification record, listing details, condition, and comparable sales before buying, selling, grading, insuring, or valuing an item.

ManeFlow does not guarantee grading outcomes, market prices, card authenticity, liquidity, or profit. Third-party services and data providers are governed by their own terms and availability. Users may upload only content they have the right to process.

The production terms should address account responsibility, prohibited use, intellectual property, subscriptions or fees, refunds, warranty disclaimers, liability limits, termination, governing law, and dispute procedures.

This draft requires publisher/legal review before publication.
