## Subtitle / Short Description

Card pricing and collection intelligence.

## Full Description

ManeFlow helps collectors, dealers, and card shops identify trading cards, review transparent completed-sale comps, track collection and inventory value, watch price targets, and make better pricing, selling, holding, consignment, and inventory decisions.

ManeFlow is built around trust. The app shows scan confidence, cert status, image source, comp quality, value confidence, liquidity, and why a card or valuation may need manual confirmation.

For shops, ManeFlow supports inventory workflows, merchant pricing guidance, intake/show mode, offer preparation, and shop-safe data separation.

Values are estimates based on included completed-sale comps. Active BIN or asking prices are context only and are not mixed into completed-sale market value. ManeFlow is not an appraisal, authentication service, grading opinion, guarantee, tax advisor, legal advisor, or investment advisor. Public live-market claims require authorized completed-sale data feeds to be connected and healthy.
