# Bulk Intake / Show Mode

Bulk Intake lets collectors and shops scan or enter cards rapidly, then review and act later.

Sources:

- show
- walk-in
- collection buy
- consignment
- trade night
- online intake

Statuses:

- draft
- reviewing
- offer_sent
- accepted
- rejected
- consigned
- inventoried

Each batch can produce an internal offer sheet based on Merchant Pricing Center guidance.
