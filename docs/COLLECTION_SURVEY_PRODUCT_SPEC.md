# Collection Survey Product Specification

**Customer line:** “Photograph the room. Understand the collection.”

Collection Survey extends ManeFlow without replacing its image-first card-identification identity. It supports collection purchase, personal collection, storage unit, card room, store inventory, show case, breaker inventory, estate, insurance documentation, and other survey types.

Current implemented output includes image count and quality warnings, unique/duplicate object review, low/expected/high card counts, confidence, evidence, assumptions, hidden-area warnings, suggested next scans, valuation evidence tiers, and a human-review requirement.

The workflow must never present a closed container as precisely counted or valued. Values remain estimates and are not formal appraisals.
