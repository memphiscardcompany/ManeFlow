# Revolutionary Pricing Intelligence

ManeFlow v1.9 focuses on the product idea that matters most: trustworthy card values should be explainable and actionable.

The app should not merely show a number. It should show:

- which sales were included
- which sales were excluded
- why a comp was trusted or rejected
- whether the source is authorized
- whether the match is exact enough
- whether the value is liquid enough to act on
- what a collector or shop should do next

## Differentiator

ManeFlow combines:

- camera scan confidence
- source-labeled completed-sale data
- Comp Quality scoring
- valuation confidence
- portfolio intelligence
- shop inventory intelligence
- merchant buy/list/consign guidance
- public-safe website embeds

The strategic edge is **trust + transparency + actionability**.

v1.9 turns that edge into the core flow: scan confidence, graded cert evidence, comp-quality reasoning, dealer-grade action guidance, and owner data operations are now visible where users actually make decisions.

## Data Honesty

Demo comps are not public market values. Active listings are not completed sales. Production claims require authorized data feeds, official APIs, seller-authorized data, partner feeds, or validated user CSV imports.
