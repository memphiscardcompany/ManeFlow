# Merchant Pricing Center

The Merchant Pricing Center converts valuation into shop action.

For a card, it returns:

- estimated market value
- confidence score
- pricing confidence score
- current BIN asking-price context
- pricing rating
- liquidity score
- comp-quality summary
- dealer buy range
- quick-sale price
- fair-list price
- patient-list price
- consignment recommendation
- grading review recommendation
- risk warnings
- suggested action

Current BIN listings can influence fair-list, patient-list, and new-release starter listing guidance. They do not create market value and do not create cash-buy targets when completed-sale value is missing.

Advanced dealer decision tools are Merchant/Enterprise gated. All prices are estimates and not appraisals or guarantees.
