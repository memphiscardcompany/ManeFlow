# Product positioning

## Category

Camera-first card price intelligence and collection operating system.

## Promise

ManeFlow turns a card in your hand into a transparent, source-backed decision: what it is, what verified buyers recently paid, how the market is moving, how confident the estimate is, and what to do next.

## Differentiation

- Exact-card identity before price
- Completed sales separated from active asks
- All-in prices including disclosed premiums and shipping
- Source, freshness, and confidence shown instead of hidden
- One action layer for collection, watch, listing, buy offer, and consignment
- Collector and merchant workflows in the same product
- Show-floor speed and offline last-known values

## Product principle

ManeFlow should never win by displaying the most data. It should win by helping the user trust the right data and act faster.
