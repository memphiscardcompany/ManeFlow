# Plan entitlements

ManeFlow enforces plan capabilities server-side. Client labels are not the security boundary.

| Plan | Vault items | Scans/day | History | Merchant tools | API |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Free | 250 | 50 | 90 days | No | No |
| Collector | 10,000 | 500 | 365 days | No | No |
| Merchant | 100,000 | 5,000 | 10 years | Yes | Yes |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | 10 years | Yes | Yes |

The owner control room can assign plans for beta users and partners. Live self-service checkout is deliberately disabled until the owner billing account, taxes, refunds, subscription cancellation, and Apple/Google purchase policies are configured.
