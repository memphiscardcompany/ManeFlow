Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

ManeFlow is a Memphis Card Company product. This policy explains how the service handles account, collection, card-image, marketplace, and usage information.

Information we collect

We may collect account details such as name and email; collection records such as card identity, purchase price, storage location, certification number, and notes; card images that you choose to submit; authorized marketplace exports; device and security metadata; and support or consignment requests.

How information is used

Information is used to provide card identification, price intelligence, collection management, alerts, imports, listing preparation, account security, support, and service improvement. Card images are processed only after you submit a scan. ManeFlow does not ask for marketplace passwords inside the app.

Service providers and data sources

Information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics, email, image-understanding, and authorized marketplace providers needed to operate the service. Marketplace data is accepted only through official APIs, written licenses, approved partnerships, or user-authorized exports.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the platform. We use access controls, secure sessions, encryption in transit, restricted secrets, request limits, and audit records. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may export your collection from the app. Account deletion, correction, or privacy requests can be sent to memphiscardcompany@gmail.com. Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, taxes, disputes, or law.

Children

ManeFlow is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by a child without a parent or guardian as permitted by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material revisions are published.
